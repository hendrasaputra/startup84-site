A business you can see.

Every number in Startup '84 is something happening in a shop. A long queue means you need another barista. An empty counter means you ran out. A price floating up from the till is a sale.

Your shops keep trading while you are away. Come back a few times a day, read the newspaper, make the day's decision, and choose where the next shop goes.

Six cities and your home town

Every city has its own streets.

Three shops in a city earn you an expansion. Spend it on a new city, each with its own buildings, cafés and customers.

Four kinds of shop

Each one plays differently.

Café

Where it starts. Machines, baristas, beans and a queue to keep moving.

Bakery

Bake in the morning, sell by the close. What is left is thrown away, so choose how much to bake.

Cake shop

Few customers, high prices. Trade follows your reputation, and it sells little until you hire a pastry chef.

Cookie kiosk

Cheap to run and fits on a pitch beside the shops. Small, fast and everywhere.

How a day plays

A few minutes, a few times a day.

  1. Run the shop

    Buy what the shop is short of. The advisor says what is holding it back and why.

  2. Hire managers

    Train them to run shops for you, so the chain grows without more tapping.

  3. Read the close

    Every day ends with a newspaper: sales, wages, rent and stock, and one decision to make.

  4. Come back richer

    Shops trade while you are away. Cash can also run out, and shops in the red close.
