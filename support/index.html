Support

Startup '84 for iPhone and iPad

Questions, bugs or ideas: write to tesbundling@gmail.com. Include your device and the game's version, shown at the top left of the screen (for example CORP/OS 1.0.0).

Common questions

Does my company keep going while I am away? Yes. Your shops trade while the game is closed, up to a limit, and the game shows what happened when you return.

How do I move my company to another device? Turn on iCloud in the game's settings on both devices. The company that has got further is kept.

The rewarded video says there is no video. Ads are not always available. Try again later; nothing is lost.

I bought a time skip and did not get it. Open the game again: an unfinished purchase is delivered at launch. If it still has not arrived, write to us.
