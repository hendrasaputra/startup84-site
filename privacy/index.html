Privacy Policy

Startup '84 · Last updated 4 November 2026

Startup '84 is a game by Hendra Saputra. It has no accounts and no login. Your company is saved on your device, and in your own iCloud if you turn that on.

What is collected

The game uses two services from Google:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) shows the optional rewarded videos. It may collect your device's advertising identifier, your approximate location from your IP address, and data about the ads you see.
  • Google Firebase Analytics records what happens in the game: for example when you open it, finish the tutorial, open a shop, or make a purchase. It may collect your advertising identifier.

Neither is linked to your name, email or any account, because the game has none.

Tracking

The game asks once, through the iOS prompt, whether apps may track you. If you allow it, the advertising identifier may be used to show you relevant ads and to measure and target advertising for the game. If you do not allow it, ads are not personalised and analytics are not used for advertising. You can change your answer at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the game first shows Google's consent form. You can consent, refuse, or manage your options, and the game plays the same either way. To change your answer later, open HELP in the game and choose PRIVACY CHOICES.

Purchases

The optional time skip is bought through Apple. The game never sees your payment details.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Your choices

  • Turn off tracking in iOS Settings.
  • Delete the game to remove its data from your device.
  • Ask for analytics data to be deleted by writing to the address below.
